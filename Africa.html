Who made this and how to cite it

This dashboard summarizes published evidence on the quality of antimalarials and maternal, newborn and child health medicines in Africa. It is based on a systematic review and meta-analysis of 123 studies (36,069 samples, 34 countries) published from 2010 through 2025, by the BESAFE team at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health.

How to cite: Rusatira JC, Dorado Novoa M, Uwimana JB, et al. (2026). Nearly one in five essential medicines fail quality standards in Africa: A systematic review and meta-analysis of MNCH medicines and antimalarials. PLOS Global Public Health 6(9): e0006326. doi:10.1371/journal.pgph.0006326
